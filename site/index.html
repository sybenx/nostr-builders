Nostr Builders

People building on Nostr, each with a Nostr profile you can follow. Found through git repos announced on Nostr (NIP-34) and the most-starred GitHub projects tagged nostr whose README names an npub. Rebuilt daily.

How it's ranked. By default, by how many other builders follow someone, plus repo activity, recent posting and GitHub stars. With your network, "followed by" counts the people you follow instead. Your follows are fetched by your own browser from public relays; nothing is sent anywhere else.

How people are linked to projects. "Nostr git" repos were announced by that profile's key. "GitHub" repos name the profile's npub in their README: usually the maintainer or the project's own account, sometimes a contributor.
